Partners in progress

Empowering farmers at scale

SBL’s supply chain begins at the farm — and the strength of our operations depends on the stability, dignity and prosperity of the farmers we work with.

  • Direct procurementFrom 8,000+ farmers, reducing dependency on intermediaries.
  • Fair, transparent pricingAligned with quality and market realities.
  • Zero exploitative credit cyclesNo forced borrowing, no tied financing.
  • Predictable offtakeIncome stability across seasons.

By ensuring consistent demand and ethical procurement, SBL enables farmers to plan better, invest confidently and sustain their livelihoods — a mutually reinforcing value chain rather than a transactional one.

A living laboratory

SBL Farms represents the Group’s long-term commitment to responsible agriculture, innovation and environmental stewardship — a place to experiment, learn and scale sustainable practices.

  • Dairy & animal husbandry

    Built on ethical, small-batch and welfare-conscious models.

  • Horticulture

    Initiatives spanning flowers, fruits, vegetables and high-value crops.

  • Aquatic plant TC lab

    Serving South-East Asian export markets.

  • Controlled agricultural experiments

    For improved farming practices.

  • Agri-tourism experiences

    Tractor rides, rain dance, swimming pool, nursery visits, pottery making and nature-based activities.

These initiatives help SBL reduce sourcing risk, improve quality predictability and build future-ready agricultural capabilities — while remaining respectful of land and natural resources.

Sustainability & green footprint

Efficiency today. Responsibility tomorrow.

Efficiency drives sustainability at SBL through smarter processes, optimised operations and responsible resource management.

01

Efficiency

By improving processing controls, integrating backend systems and automating processes, the Group has reduced waste, optimised energy use and minimised material losses in centralised processing and storage.

A company-owned logistics fleet further reduces redundant transport movements, improving turnaround times while minimising resource intensity per tonne processed.

02

Environment

Disciplined, practical actions rather than short-term claims. Centralised manufacturing, improved yield management and controlled sourcing help reduce avoidable waste and emissions across the supply chain.

Continuous investments in technology ensure cleaner processing, better utilisation of inputs and consistent quality.

03

Future

Future facilities, including the upcoming integrated food park, are being designed to operate with a lower environmental load per tonne processed, improved resource efficiency and higher automation.

Aligning SBL with the evolving ESG expectations of global customers and institutional partners.
