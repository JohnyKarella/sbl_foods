The story

More than a business — a legacy of trust

I

The roots

The roots of this legacy were laid in the pre-independence era by his father, Shri B. S. Seetharam Shetty, whose family business was built on integrity, hard work and community-driven enterprise.

Quality, consistency and trust would form the foundation of the Indian food industry.

II

The vision

Shri B. S. Amaranarayana Gupta was a forward-thinking entrepreneur. When food processing in India was still in its early stages, he envisioned a future in which quality, consistency and trust would form the foundation of the Indian food industry.

Carrying his father’s values forward, he formally established Sri Bhagyalakshmi Industries, marking the group’s entry into organised agro and food processing.

III

The growth

Drawing on extensive agricultural expertise and a dedication to high-quality food, he steadily expanded the business into pulse, rice and wheat processing — foreseeing the shifting demands of an increasingly urban India.

What began as a small venture has evolved into a well-regarded food brand offering more than 110 trusted products, with a solid footprint across South India.

IV

The values

For decades, his principles of ethical business, unwavering quality and commitment to serving society have remained the central mindset of the organisation. Grounded in tradition and propelled by purpose, his legacy continues to instil a dedication to quality, authenticity and trust in every product that reaches the consumer’s home.

  • Ethical business
  • Unwavering quality
  • Serving society
