Core Trading & Raw Material Procurement

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Trading Corporation

Supply Foundation

A renowned trading hub within the group, overseeing the procurement and trading of food grains, cereals, pulses and flours — including the import of select cereals and pulses from the USA, Canada and African regions.

Core Trading & Raw Material Procurement

Amar Foods

Supply Foundation

Strengthens the group’s raw material ecosystem through the procurement of high-quality paddy directly from key farming regions, fostering long-term relationships with farming communities.

Specialised Food Processing & Manufacturing

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Agro Foods Pvt. Ltd.

Production Engine

Specialised Food Processing & Manufacturing

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Enterprises

Production Engine

Specialised Food Processing & Manufacturing

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Commercial Corporation

Production Engine

Distribution, Institutional Supply & Export

Gupta Foods

Market Access

Distribution, Institutional Supply & Export

Food Valley Impex, Mumbai

Market Access

Located close to India’s key export logistics hubs — rice cleaning, polishing, export packaging and containerised shipments with export-ready quality standards.

Global Expansion, Agriculture & Future Infrastructure

Future Growth

A flagship infrastructure initiative near Bengaluru — a large integrated food processing ecosystem with manufacturing units, packaging lines, silos, cold storage and logistics.

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Farms

Future Growth

Nyagatare Agro Ventures Rwanda Pvt. Ltd.

Future Growth

The group’s international venture in Rwanda, integrating mechanised farming with local food processing — sourcing, cleaning, processing and trading of food grains.
