Who We Are

Goodness Built on Generations of Trust

For over five decades, SBL Foods has grown with one simple belief — trust is earned through consistency. What began as a family-led food enterprise has evolved into one of South India’s most dependable food processing groups.

Today the group processes and supplies a large variety of food grains, flour and pulses to distributors, large retailers, hotels, restaurants, caterers, institutions and branded supermarkets — with quality engineered into every stage, from sourcing and milling to testing, packing and delivery.

  • Roots in the pre-independence era, since 1947
  • Four state-of-the-art plants in Bengaluru
  • Labs run by qualified food technologists
  • Technical collaboration with CFTRI, Mysore

How the Ecosystem Creates Value

From Farm to Fork

An integrated network gives SBL control at every step — sourcing, processing, testing, packing and logistics.

  1. 01

    Farm Sourcing

    Direct procurement from 8,000+ farmers, paddy from key farming regions, and select cereals and pulses imported from the USA, Canada and Africa.

  2. 02

    Processing

    Four plants in Bengaluru with Buhler & Sortex technology and fully automated processing, running at 800+ tonnes a day.

  3. 03

    Quality Control

    Raw material testing, in-house paddy testing and physical, microbial & chemical analysis by qualified food technologists.

  4. 04

    Packaging

    Hygienic smart packing — with packaging upgraded over the years from gunny bags to laminated materials.

  5. 05

    Distribution

    A company-owned fleet of 100–150 cargo vehicles, from 3-wheelers to 12-wheelers, keeps full control over timelines.

  6. 06

    Retail · HoReCa · Exports

    350+ distributors across 235+ locations, with export facilitation through Food Valley Impex, Mumbai.

Founder’s Legacy

More Than Five Decades of Nourishing Generations

“A Vision That Nourishes Generations.”

Shri B. S. Amaranarayana Gupta was a forward-thinking entrepreneur who turned a family enterprise into one of South India’s most reliable food businesses. When food processing in India was still in its early stages, he envisioned a future in which quality, consistency and trust would form the foundation of the Indian food industry.

Building on the foundation laid by his father, Shri B. S. Seetharam Shetty, he expanded the business into pulse, rice and wheat processing — foreseeing the shifting demands of an increasingly urban India.
